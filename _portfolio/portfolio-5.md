* Built a reproducible spatial-data workflow in Python (Scanpy, AnnData) — quality control, normalization,
  feature selection, PCA, nearest-neighbor graph construction, and pseudo-spatial coordinate generation
* Implemented and benchmarked three cell-type deconvolution methods — linear least squares, CARD, and DestVI —
  validating outputs with residual diagnostics and comparison metrics
* Produced georeferenced spatial maps, heatmaps, UMAP visualizations, and cell-type proportion summaries
* Columbia University, 2025 — code: [github.com/st3759/5243-Final-Report-2025FAll](https://github.com/st3759/5243-Final-Report-2025FAll)
