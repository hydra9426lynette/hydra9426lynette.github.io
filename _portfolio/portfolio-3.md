Analyzed high-dimensional DNA-sequencing data in R — quality-control assessment and visualization of sequencing
reads, genetic variation, and structural changes.

Developed R-based simulations to evaluate mathematical and statistical methods for interpreting genetic
mechanisms and disease pathways, applying Approximate Bayesian Computation with random forests and sequential
Monte Carlo methods to infer parameters and quantify uncertainty in high-dimensional genetic datasets.

**Skills:** R, Approximate Bayesian Computation, random forests, sequential Monte Carlo, Bayesian statistics
